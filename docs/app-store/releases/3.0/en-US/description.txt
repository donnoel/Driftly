Make room for a quieter moment.

Driftly Night turns your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV into a living canvas of light, color, and slow motion. Set it beside your bed, keep it on your desk, or fill a larger screen with a gentle atmosphere.

DISCOVER LIVING VISUALS
Watch koi glide, rain trace a window, luminous blossoms unfold, and ribbons flow. Explore abstract light, opal-like cells, colorful gardens, and little animated companions that reward an unhurried look.

MAKE IT YOUR OWN
Choose a favorite visual, adjust brightness and animation speed, and save named scenes for moments you want to return to. Mark favorites and let Auto Drift move between visuals at your chosen interval.

SETTLE IN
Use the sleep timer when it is time for the visuals to rest, or add a clock for a quiet nightstand display. Simple controls blend into the atmosphere so the visuals have room to breathe.

FROM A SMALL SCREEN TO YOUR ROOM
Enjoy Driftly Night on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. Favorites can sync through iCloud, and the Apple TV remote guide helps you find your way around.

A little color. A slower pace. A space to unwind.
